# Kairo homepage artwork

The homepage uses original artwork generated with the built-in imagegen tool, not Railway's imagery.

Saved asset: `public/images/kairo-observatory.png`.

The interface takes visual inspiration from Railway's current homepage: generous typography, a calm illustrated hero, a short product narrative, and clear entry into the application. All copy and artwork were created for Kairo. The product preview is explicitly illustrative; actual experiments run in the existing workspace at `/app`.

## Final generation prompt

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: panoramic website hero background for Kairo, a scientific research platform. Create an original atmospheric hand-painted landscape, wide 16:9 composition, with a small white observatory dome and low modern research laboratory on a grassy hill in the LOWER RIGHT quarter, a curving path leading to it, distant layered blue mountains and a lake, golden wild grasses in foreground. The TOP TWO THIRDS is expansive soft pale blue sky, nearly empty in center, with gorgeous large peach and cream cumulus clouds at the far left and right edges, softly brushed texture, warm early morning sunlight. Sophisticated cinematic animation background painting, calm and quietly optimistic, subtle painterly detail and film grain, muted slate blue, pale celadon, soft coral and warm ivory. View at human eye level from a distance. This is a BACKGROUND image: central upper sky must be very light and low detail so dark headline text placed in HTML will read clearly. Keep scenery in bottom third. No train, no railway tracks, no people, no UI, no text, no typography, no logos, no watermarks.
